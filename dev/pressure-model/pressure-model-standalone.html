Chamber Pressure

Hair-color frequencies from HairEyeColor (N = 592) as molecules of gas in four chambers. Partition heights fit by maximum likelihood give equal density everywhere — the gas is at rest. Force the partitions to the hypothesis of equal proportions (H₀: π = ¼ each) and some chambers get compressed, others vented. The pressure it takes is G², twice the work done against the data.
